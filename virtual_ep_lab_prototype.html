Quick learning notes

A few concepts to help you read the simulated trace.

01
Rhythm

Rhythm describes the pattern and spacing of heartbeats. A trace can look regular or irregular, but rhythm identification requires more than one visual clue.

02
ECG and intracardiac signals are different

An ECG records electrical activity from electrodes on the skin. Intracardiac electrograms record signals from electrodes inside the heart during specialized procedures.

03
Patterns need context

Real rhythm interpretation uses multiple leads, signal timing, symptoms, clinical context and expert judgment. This demo intentionally teaches only a basic visual concept.
